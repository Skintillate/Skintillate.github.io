# Content review checklist for Dr. Kiruthika

All treatment pages were drafted for the website and need your medical review before launch. Please read each page and mark any changes. To read the pages, open the files in `services/` or the live preview.

## For every treatment page, please check

- [ ] Every treatment, procedure and laser mentioned is actually offered at the clinic.
- [ ] Medical facts are correct: session numbers, intervals, downtime, how long results last.
- [ ] Nothing reads as a guarantee or a superlative, and nothing conflicts with NMC advertising guidelines.
- [ ] The FAQs reflect how you would answer patients.

Once a page is approved, set `last_reviewed:` in its front matter to the approval date.
