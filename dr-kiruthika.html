About Dr. Kiruthika

Dr. Kiruthika completed her MBBS at Madras Medical College, Chennai, followed by an MD in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology from the prestigious PGIMER, Chandigarh, and a DNB in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology from the National Board of Examinations, New Delhi.

She has served as a Senior Resident at both JIPMER, Puducherry and PGIMER, Chandigarh, gaining extensive experience in managing a wide range of skin, hair and nail conditions.

Her academic and research interests include dermatologic surgery and advanced aesthetic procedures, and her research has received medals at national conferences. She has been invited as faculty at national and international conferences and has authored research publications and textbook chapters.

With a commitment to evidence-based, personalised care, Dr. Kiruthika brings together precision in treatment, artistry in aesthetics and compassion in patient care, helping every person achieve healthier skin and renewed confidence.
