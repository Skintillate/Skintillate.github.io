## Botulinum toxin (Botox)

Botulinum toxin relaxes the small muscles that cause expression lines, smoothing the skin above them. It is commonly used for:

- Forehead lines
- Frown lines between the eyebrows
- Crow's feet around the eyes
- Excessive sweating of the underarms, palms or feet (hyperhidrosis)

The treatment takes about 15 minutes with very fine needles. Results appear within a week and last around 3 to 4 months.

## Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid fillers are gels that restore lost volume, support sagging areas and smooth deep folds. They are used for:

- Under-eye hollows (tear troughs)
- Cheeks and midface volume
- Smile lines (nasolabial folds)
- Lips
- Chin and jawline definition

Depending on the area and product, results last from about 6 to 18 months. Because hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved if needed, they are a reversible option.

## Skin rejuvenation

Not every concern needs injections. Treatments that improve skin quality, tone and texture include:

- **Skin boosters**: injectable hyaluronic acid that hydrates the skin from within for a natural glow.
- **Microneedling**, with or without PRP, to stimulate collagen and refine texture.
- **[Fractional CO₂ laser](/services/laser-treatment/)** for fine lines, pores and uneven texture.
- **[Chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels/)** and **HydraFacial** for brightness and smoothness.
- **Prescription creams** such as retinoids, together with daily sunscreen, the foundation of any anti-ageing plan.

## Safety first

- A detailed assessment of your face, skin and goals before any treatment.
- Approved, medical-grade products only.
- Injections given by a dermatologist with a thorough knowledge of facial anatomy.
- Treatments are postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Tell us about any medicines you take, especially blood thinners.
