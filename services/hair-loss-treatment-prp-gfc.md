## Common causes of hair loss

- **Pattern hair loss** in men (receding hairline, thinning crown) and women (widening parting).
- **Telogen effluvium**: sudden heavy shedding a few months after fever, illness, surgery, childbirth, stress or crash dieting.
- **Nutritional deficiencies** such as low iron, vitamin D, vitamin B12 or protein.
- **Hormonal causes**, including thyroid problems and PCOS.
- **Alopecia areata**: round, smooth bald patches on the scalp, beard or eyebrows.
- **Scalp conditions** such as dandruff, seborrhoeic dermatitis, psoriasis and fungal infection.
- **Hair care practices**: tight hairstyles, frequent heat styling, chemical straightening and colouring.
- **Medicines** that can trigger hair fall.

## Finding the cause

Dr. Kiruthika takes a detailed history and closely examines your scalp and hair. Blood tests are advised when a deficiency or hormonal cause is suspected. Most people have more than one contributing factor, and treating all of them gives the best result.

## Treatment options

### Medicines
Topical and oral treatments such as minoxidil and, for suitable patients, other prescription medicines. Nutritional deficiencies are corrected with supplements.

### PRP (platelet-rich plasma) therapy
A small amount of your blood is drawn and spun to separate platelet-rich plasma, which is rich in growth factors. It is then injected into the thinning areas of the scalp to support hair growth. A typical course is 3 to 4 sessions about a month apart, followed by maintenance sessions as advised.

### GFC (growth factor concentrate) therapy
GFC is also prepared from your own blood. The platelets are activated so they release their growth factors, which are concentrated and injected into the scalp. Sessions follow a similar schedule to PRP.

### Intralesional injections for alopecia areata
Medicine injected directly into bald patches to help hair regrow. [Read more about intralesional injections](/services/intralesional-injections/).

### Scalp treatment
Dandruff and scalp inflammation are treated with medicated shampoos, lotions and, where needed, oral medicines.

### Hair transplant consultation
If your hair loss is advanced, Dr. Kiruthika can assess whether a hair transplant is suitable and guide you on the next steps.
