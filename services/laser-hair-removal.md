## How the diode laser works

The laser light is absorbed by the pigment in the hair and turned into heat, which damages the hair follicle and reduces its ability to grow new hair. The surrounding skin is protected by built-in cooling.

Laser works best on dark, coarse hair. White, grey, red and very fine light hair contains little pigment and responds poorly.

## Why you need several sessions

Hair grows in cycles, and the laser only affects hairs that are actively growing at the time of treatment. Most people need **6 to 8 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart**, to treat each hair during its growth phase. The face sometimes needs sessions at shorter intervals.

## Why see a dermatologist

Excess facial or body hair in women can be a sign of a hormonal condition such as PCOS. Dr. Kiruthika looks for and treats underlying causes as part of your plan, which gives better and longer-lasting results than laser alone. Laser settings are also adjusted to your skin to avoid burns and pigment changes.

## Before and after your session

**Before**
- Shave the area the day before your session.
- Stop waxing, threading and plucking for 4 weeks before.
- Avoid sun tanning and bleaching for 2 weeks before.

**After**
- Mild redness and small bumps around the hairs usually settle within a few hours.
- Use sunscreen and avoid direct sun on treated areas.
- Treated hairs shed over 1 to 3 weeks.
