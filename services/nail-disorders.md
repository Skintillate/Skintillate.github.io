## Nail problems we treat

- **Fungal nail infection** (onychomycosis): thick, yellow, white or crumbly nails, usually on the toes.
- **Paronychia**: painful swelling around the nail, common in people whose hands are often wet, such as homemakers and cooks.
- **Ingrown toenails**: a painful, red, sometimes infected nail edge digging into the skin.
- **Nail psoriasis and lichen planus**: pitting, ridges, thickening or nail separation.
- **Brittle, splitting or peeling nails.**
- **Discolouration and lines**, including dark streaks that need to be examined.
- **Warts around the nails.**
- **Nail changes linked to general health**, such as anaemia, thyroid disease or diabetes.

## Diagnosis

Many nail conditions look alike. A fungal infection, for example, can look very similar to nail psoriasis. Dr. Kiruthika examines your nails and, when needed, sends a nail clipping or scraping for a fungal test before starting long courses of treatment.

## Treatment

- **Antifungal medicines** (tablets, nail lacquers and creams) for fungal infections.
- **Treatment of paronychia** with medicines and advice on keeping the hands dry.
- **A minor procedure for ingrown toenails** under local anaesthesia, to remove the ingrown edge and reduce the chance of recurrence.
- **[Intralesional injections](/services/intralesional-injections/)** for nail psoriasis and other inflammatory nail conditions.
- **[Electrosurgery](/services/electrosurgery-wart-skin-tag-removal/)** and other treatments for warts around the nails.
- **Nail care advice** for brittle nails, including protection from water and harsh chemicals.

## Nails take time

Because nails grow slowly, improvement is gradual. Expect to see the new, healthy nail growing out from the base over several months. Regular follow-up visits help make sure treatment is working.
