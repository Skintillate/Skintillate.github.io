## Types of acne we treat

- Blackheads and whiteheads (comedonal acne)
- Red, painful pimples and pus-filled bumps
- Deep, painful nodules and cysts
- Adult and hormonal acne, including acne linked to PCOS
- Acne on the back, chest and shoulders
- Acne triggered by cosmetics, hair oils, steroid creams or medicines

## How to get rid of pimples and active acne

At your first visit, Dr. Kiruthika examines your skin and asks about the products you use, any medicines you take and, where relevant, your menstrual cycle. She then grades the severity of your acne and plans treatment around it. Your plan may include:

- **Creams and gels** such as retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, azelaic acid or topical antibiotics.
- **Oral medicines** for moderate to severe acne, such as antibiotics, isotretinoin or hormonal treatment, when appropriate.
- **Comedone extraction**, done hygienically in the clinic.
- **[Chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels/)**, for example salicylic acid peels, to unclog pores and speed up improvement.
- **A simple skin-care routine**: a gentle cleanser, a non-comedogenic moisturiser and daily sunscreen.

## Treating acne marks and scars

Not every mark that acne leaves behind is a scar. Flat brown or red marks usually fade with treatment, while true scars change the texture of the skin. Scar treatment starts once active acne is under control.

| Type | What it looks like | Treatments that may help |
|---|---|---|
| Dark or red marks | Flat brown, red or purple spots | Creams, chemical peels |
| Rolling scars | Wide, wave-like dips | Subcision, microneedling, fractional CO₂ laser |
| Boxcar scars | Broad dips with sharp edges | Fractional CO₂ laser, microneedling, peels |
| Ice-pick scars | Narrow, deep pits | Focal TCA (CROSS) technique |
| Raised or keloid scars | Firm, thick bumps | [Intralesional injections](/services/intralesional-injections/) |
{: .scar-table}

### Subcision

A fine needle is passed under rolling scars to release the bands that pull the skin down, done under local anaesthesia.

### Microneedling

Tiny controlled punctures stimulate the skin to produce new collagen, gradually smoothing scars. It can be combined with PRP.

### Fractional CO₂ laser

The laser resurfaces the skin in microscopic columns, encouraging new collagen and smoother texture. Expect a few days of redness and fine crusting after each session.

Most people need several sessions, usually 4 to 6 weeks apart, and a combination of methods works better than any single one.
