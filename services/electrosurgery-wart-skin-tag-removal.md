## What can be removed

- **Skin tags** on the neck, underarms, eyelids and groin.
- **Warts**: common warts, flat warts, plantar warts on the soles, and warts around the nails.
- **Dermatosis papulosa nigra (DPN)**: small dark bumps on the face and neck, very common in Indian skin.
- **Moles**, after examination to confirm they are harmless.
- **Seborrhoeic keratoses**: raised, waxy brown growths that appear with age.
- **Milia, syringomas** and other small benign bumps.

## What are electrosurgery and electrofulguration?

**Electrosurgery** uses a high-frequency electrical current delivered through a fine tip to precisely remove or destroy unwanted tissue. The heat seals tiny blood vessels at the same time, so there is very little bleeding.

**Electrofulguration** is a gentler form in which fine sparks are applied just above the skin surface. It is ideal for very superficial growths such as small skin tags and DPN.

## What to expect

1. **Examination.** Dr. Kiruthika confirms what each growth is. Anything unusual is assessed first and may need a biopsy.
2. **Numbing.** A numbing cream or local anaesthetic injection is used.
3. **Procedure.** Each growth takes a few seconds to a few minutes to remove.
4. **Healing.** Small scabs form and fall off on their own in about 7 to 10 days.

### Aftercare
- Keep the area clean and apply the ointment prescribed.
- Do not pick at the scabs.
- Use sunscreen on treated areas to reduce darkening.
- You can usually return to normal activities the same day.

## Safety first

Not every growth should simply be burnt off. A mole that is changing in size, shape or colour, has irregular edges, itches or bleeds should be examined by a dermatologist, and sometimes tested, before any treatment. This is why every removal at Skintillate starts with a proper examination.
