## Types of pigmentation we treat

- **Melasma.** Brown or grey-brown patches on the cheeks, forehead, nose or upper lip, often linked to sun, heat, pregnancy or hormonal medicines.
- **Dark marks after acne, injury or rashes** (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation).
- **Sun spots, freckles and tanning.**
- **Dark circles** under the eyes.
- **Dark, velvety skin on the neck, underarms or knuckles** (acanthosis nigricans), which can be linked to insulin resistance, weight or PCOS.
- **Pigmentation around the mouth and lips.**
- **Pigmented birthmarks** such as nevus of Ota.
- **Pigmentation from cosmetics, hair dye or medicines.**

White patches are a different problem; see [clinical dermatology](/services/clinical-dermatology/) for vitiligo and other causes.

## How pigmentation is treated

Most people do best with a combination of treatments, adjusted as the skin improves.

### Sun protection
The foundation of every pigmentation plan. Without it, other treatments work poorly and results do not last.

### Prescription creams
Ingredients such as azelaic acid, kojic acid, retinoids, niacinamide and, for short supervised courses, hydroquinone combinations. These are chosen for your skin type and used under follow-up.

### Oral medicines
For selected people with melasma, tablets such as tranexamic acid may be added after a health check.

### Chemical peels
Glycolic, lactic, mandelic, salicylic and combination peels lift away pigmented surface cells. [Read more about chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels/).

### Q-switched Nd:YAG laser
Laser toning breaks up excess pigment into tiny particles that the body clears away. It is used for melasma, freckles, sun spots and birthmarks such as nevus of Ota. [Read more about our lasers](/services/laser-treatment/).

### HydraFacial and medi-facials
Gentle cleansing and hydrating treatments that brighten the skin and support the main treatment plan.

## Melasma needs patience

Melasma is a long-term condition. Treatment can lighten it considerably, but sun, heat and hormones can bring it back. A realistic aim is good control, with a simple maintenance routine once your skin has cleared.
